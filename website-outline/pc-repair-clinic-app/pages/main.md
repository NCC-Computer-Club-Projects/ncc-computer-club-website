## Objective

Answer the question: **"What can the PC Repair Clinic do for me?"** What should users see when they first open the app? The main page sets customer expectations, so keep this in mind when designing its layout and presentation.

## Header and Background

Display the PC Repair Clinic logo with a fixed background image of the clinic.

## Services

Provide sample images or icons representing the clinic's areas of specialization. Include at least the following:

### Request a Repair

Submit a detailed description of a repair request. This is the primary option for requesting repairs.

### Quick Advising

Submit a description of the device and issue. A PC Repair Clinic volunteer will respond with a repair recommendation. This provides an alternative to visiting the clinic in person.

### Schedule a Consultation Appointment

Schedule a consultation appointment with a PC Repair Clinic volunteer.

### Repair Quick Tips

Provide general troubleshooting tips for common issues for customers who do not want to visit the clinic or wait for a repair.

## Our Hours

Display the PC Repair Clinic's operating hours at the bottom of the page.

## Status

Show the current progress of the user's repair request:

- Stage 1: Request received
- Stage 2: Repair in progress
- Stage 3: Repair completed; ready for pickup
