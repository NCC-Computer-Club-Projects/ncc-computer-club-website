## Our Mission  

We, the students of Northampton Community College's computer and information technology sector, learn many skills through our studies but sometimes struggle to find practical applications for those skills outside the classroom. Our members have the potential to create meaningful projects for themselves and the community using what they learn at Northampton Community College, as well as skills developed through independent research and hard work.

The Computer Club gives students opportunities to put their knowledge into practice through competitions, projects, and community involvement. The club also connects students with valuable resources, including certification preparation, portfolio development, exam preparation, interview practice, and other career-building opportunities.

Through vision and collaboration, the members of the Northampton Community College Computer Club are committed to helping one another on the journey toward computer wizardry.
